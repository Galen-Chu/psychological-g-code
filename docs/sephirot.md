# Kabbalah Tree of Life Reference

## Overview

The **Tree of Life (Etz Chaim)** is the central diagram of Kabbalah, representing the ten emanations (sephirot) through which the Infinite (Ein Sof) reveals and creates the universe. Each sephira represents an aspect of consciousness, divine attribute, and level of reality.

## Structure

### Three Pillars
- **Pillar of Mercy (Right)**: Force, expansion, masculine (Chokhmah, Chesed, Netzach)
- **Pillar of Severity (Left)**: Form, constriction, feminine (Binah, Gevurah, Hod)
- **Pillar of Balance (Middle)**: Consciousness, integration (Keter, Tiferet, Yesod, Malkuth)

### Four Worlds
1. **Atziluth** (Archetypal World) - Keter, Chokhmah, Binah
2. **Beriyah** (Creative World) - Chesed, Gevurah, Tiferet
3. **Yetzirah** (Formative World) - Netzach, Hod, Yesod
4. **Assiyah** (Physical World) - Malkuth

## The Ten Sephirot

### 1. Keter (Crown) - כתר

- **Color**: White

**Meaning**: Pure consciousness, divine will, unity, the source of all
**Psychological**: Transcendence, enlightenment, divine spark

## Key Concepts

### Da'ath (Knowledge)
Sometimes called the "hidden sephira," Da'ath is not counted among the ten but represents the point where Chokhmah and Binah unite in knowledge. It sits in the abyss (the gap between the Supernal Triad and the lower seven sephirot).

### The Lightning Flash
The path of creation: Keter → Chokhmah → Binah → Chesed → Gevurah → Tiferet → Netzach → Hod → Yesod → Malkuth

### The Serpent of Wisdom
The path of return: Malkuth → Yesod → Hod → Netzach → Tiferet → Gevurah → Chesed → Binah → Chokhmah → Keter

### Practical Applications
1. **Meditation** - On individual sephirot or paths
2. **Pathworking** - Conscious journeying along the paths
3. **Middle Pillar Exercise** - Balancing the central column
4. **Tree of Life in daily life** - Applying the sephirot as qualities

## Important Notes

### Hermetic Qabalah vs. Traditional Kabbalah
- **Traditional Kabbalah**: Jewish mystical tradition, Torah-based, requires Hebrew literacy
- **Hermetic Qabalah**: Western esoteric adaptation, incorporates astrology, tarot, ceremonial magic
- This project primarily uses the Hermetic framework, which is the source of most correspondences

### Cultural Respect
- The Tree of Life originates in Jewish mysticism
- Western adaptations have diverged significantly from the original tradition
- Respect for the source tradition is essential
- Not all Jewish Kabbalists accept these correspondences or applications
